## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that your browser stores on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage (localStorage), work in much the same way. Together they help a website function properly, remember your choices and understand how visitors use it.

Cookies can be **session cookies**, which are deleted when you close your browser, or **persistent cookies**, which remain on your device for a set period. They can also be **first-party** (set by the Site itself) or **third-party** (set by another provider whose service is used on the Site).

**The Site does not currently use analytics cookies.** If Google Analytics 4 is introduced in future, analytics cookies will **only be loaded if you give your consent** through the cookie banner. Until you do, no analytics scripts run and no analytics cookies are placed on your device.

## Managing Your Preferences

### On the Site

Once analytics cookies are introduced, a cookie banner shown on your first visit will let you accept or reject them. Rejecting is just as easy as accepting.

You can then change your mind at any time: click **"Cookie preferences"** in the footer of any page to reopen the banner and update your choice. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before you withdrew it.

### In Your Browser

Most browsers let you view, delete or block cookies and other site data through their settings or privacy menu. If you clear your browser data, the `ypg-consent` entry is deleted as well, and the cookie banner will appear again on your next visit.

You can find instructions in the help pages of your browser (for example Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Apple Safari or Microsoft Edge). If you want to opt out of Google Analytics across all websites, Google also offers a browser add-on for this purpose.

Please note that blocking strictly necessary technologies may cause parts of the Site not to work as intended.
